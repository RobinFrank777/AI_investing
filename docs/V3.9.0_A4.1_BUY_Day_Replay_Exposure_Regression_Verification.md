## Why the Initial A4 Summary Said 80%

The A4 diagnostic command used helpers from `tests/test_buy_day_replay.py`:

- a nine-row fabricated score snapshot (eight BUY plus one WATCH), rather than
  the 147-row historical cross-section;
- the same synthetic market series for all eight tickers, rather than their
  distinct canonical histories;
- supplied GOOD fundamental fixtures.

Those identical synthetic price paths generate identical risk multipliers.
Normalizing eight equal multipliers to 80% produces 10% per ticker, so no cap
creates unused cash. The test fixture correctly exercises pipeline contracts,
but it is not comparable to the P2.3 replay and must not be reported as such.

This is replay input mismatch in the diagnostic method and a transcription error
in the A4 result, not drift in production inputs or behavior.
