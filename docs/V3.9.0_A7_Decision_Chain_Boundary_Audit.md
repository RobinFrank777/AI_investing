# V3.9.0 A7 Decision Chain Boundary Audit

## Executive Summary

No Decision Chain module is a safe move-only candidate. Every module either owns
production validation/policy, reads or publishes a formal artifact, exposes a
root orchestration entry, or is a cross-chain validation replay. A7 therefore
creates no `src/decision` package and makes no Python change.

The real 2026-06-18 replay reproduced the established 76.5573770492% exposure,
all chain stages, metadata, no-look-ahead, no-redistribution, aggregate manual
review, and current-run isolation contracts.

## Decision Authority Chain

```text
FinalScore + TradeSignal
  -> Production Candidate: who may enter consideration
  -> Risk Inputs: point-in-time evidence and risk multiplier
  -> Portfolio: risk-ready selection, weights, caps, retained cash
  -> Position Sizing: target value and whole-share proposal
  -> Order Draft: proposed DRAFT_ONLY orders
  -> Order Review: mechanical/evidence PASS, REVIEW, BLOCKED
  -> Human Review: final decision authority
```

Risk does not redefine BUY authority. Sizing and orders do not authorize broker
execution.

## Metadata Contract

RunId remains single-valued across a production chain. AsOfDate remains
single-valued and current for the run. UniverseVersion and ScoreModelVersion
originate at candidate evidence. RiskModelVersion is added by the risk builder
and preserved downstream. Mixed, missing, stale, or fabricated metadata fail
closed; replay metadata does not overwrite current-run state.

## Point-in-Time Contract

- Risk market rows are limited to `MarketData.Date <= Candidate.AsOfDate`.
- `LatestCloseAsOf <= AsOfDate`.
- Adding future rows does not alter the historical replay.
