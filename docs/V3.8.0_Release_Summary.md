# AI_investing V3.8.0 Release Summary

Release Date: 2026-08-11

## 1. Release Overview

AI_investing V3.8.0 extends the V3.7.0 research foundation with an Investment
Profile system and qualitative research context. The release preserves the
existing quantitative pipeline while adding validated, human-maintained company
metadata for long-term research.

The Investment Profile system helps researchers understand what a company does,
its long-term investment thesis, competitive moat, investment stage, growth
drivers, investor rating, and major risks. This context is presented beside
quantitative outputs without changing them.

## 2. Major Features Added

### Investment Profile system

V3.8.0 adds a reusable qualitative research layer built around validated Company
Profile metadata and explicit coverage governance.

### Company Profile data model

`data/company_profile.csv` is the master data source for qualitative company
research. Its fixed schema includes company identity, industry classification,
business model, investment thesis, moat assessment, valuation type, growth and
risk context, investment stage, investor rating, and update date.

### Profile validation layer

`company_profile_validator.py` validates the Company Profile contract, including
schema order, required values, ticker uniqueness, numeric ranges and integer
requirements, allowed classifications, and update-date formatting. Invalid data
is reported through a structured validation result rather than modified
automatically.

### Investment Profile loader

`investment_profile_loader.py` provides reusable access to the validated Profile
dataset and individual ticker records. Research presentation modules consume the
loader instead of reading the Company Profile CSV directly.

### Coverage and tier management

`investment_profile_coverage.py` reports overall Universe150 and tier-level
coverage. `data/company_profile_tiers.csv` records research-priority metadata for
Profile expansion planning. Coverage metrics are governance information, not
investment scores.

### Stock Card integration

Stock Research Cards include an independent Investment Profile section with
company, business model, investment thesis, moat score, growth driver, risk
factor, investment stage, and investor rating information. Missing or invalid
Profile data does not block card generation.

### Research Terminal Long-Term Context

Top Opportunity cards in the Research Terminal include compact Long-Term
Context. The Terminal loads validated profiles once, displays qualitative
context when available, and falls back to an unavailable message when Profile
data is missing or invalid.

## 3. Architecture Boundary

### Quantitative Layer

The existing quantitative research and decision-support layer includes:

- market data
- factor calculations
- ranking
- signal generation
- risk analysis
- portfolio research

### Qualitative Research Layer

The Investment Profile layer includes:

- company profile metadata
- business model and investment thesis
- moat assessment
- investment stage and investor rating
- long-term growth and risk context

The layers meet only in research presentation. Investment Profile does **not**
affect:

- scoring
- ranking
- signals
- portfolio construction
- order review

It also does not change technical indicators, factor calculations, position
sizing, risk controls, or trading decisions.

## 7. Known Limitations

- Company Profile coverage is incomplete outside the completed Tier1 set.
- Profile metadata and tier assignments require manual maintenance and review.
- An AI analyst or AI-assisted analysis layer is not implemented.
- Automated trading and live brokerage execution are not implemented.

## 8. Future Direction

Potential future work includes:

- completing and maintaining broader Company Profile coverage
- improving qualitative research presentation and review workflows
- evaluating a separate AI-assisted research layer with explicit design,
  validation, and human-review boundaries

These items are future directions, not capabilities included in V3.8.0.
