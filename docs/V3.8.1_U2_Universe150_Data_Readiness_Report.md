# V3.8.1 U2 Universe150 Data Readiness Report

## 1. Objective

U2 audits local canonical market-data readiness for every member of the formal Universe150. It measures whether each configured ticker can enter the formal analysis chain; it does not change the Universe or make an investment decision.

## 2. Universe Contract

The authority is `data/AI_investing_universe_150_V2.csv`, version `AI_investing_universe_150_V2`.

**Universe Membership ≠ Data Readiness.** The Primary Universe remains 150 securities even though 142 are currently data-ready.

## 3. Readiness Contract

A ticker is Ready only when all of these existing production requirements pass:

- `{Ticker}.csv` exists and is parseable;
- canonical columns `Date`, `Open`, `High`, `Low`, `Close`, and `Volume` exist;
- the file contains at least 252 rows, matching the existing main-chain High252/history requirement;
- dates are valid, unique, and monotonically increasing;
- OHLCV values are numeric and finite;
- prices are positive and volume is non-negative;
- High/Low contain Open, Close, and one another under the canonical OHLC relationship checks.

Freshness is reported through `FirstDate` and `LastDate`. No new stale-data policy or investment meaning is introduced.

Stable failure reasons include `MISSING_FILE`, `PARSE_ERROR`, `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`, `DUPLICATE_DATES`, `INVALID_NUMERIC`, `INVALID_OHLC`, and `OTHER_VALIDATION_ERROR` subtypes.

## 4. Infrastructure Reused

- `universe_loader.py` supplies all configured Primary Universe tickers.
- `stock_loader.load_stock_file()` remains the canonical OHLCV validator.
- `data_readiness.py` was extended to collect diagnostics and isolate per-ticker failures.
- `update_data.update_one_stock()` remains the sole supported single-ticker downloader; no second downloader or loader was created.
