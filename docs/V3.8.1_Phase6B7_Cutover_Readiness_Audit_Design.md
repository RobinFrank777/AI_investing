# AI_investing V3.8.1 Phase 6B-7 Cutover Readiness Audit Design

Design date: 2026-08-12

Status: design only. This audit does not authorize or perform a production
cutover.

# 1. Purpose of the Cutover Readiness Audit

Phase 6B-7 defines the evidence required to decide whether the portfolio
pipeline may progress from its legacy backtest-driven candidate source toward
the validated production-candidate and point-in-time risk path.

The audit must answer four separate questions:

1. Are the new candidate and risk contracts internally valid and reproducible?
2. Have they remained stable across multiple point-in-time shadow snapshots?
3. Can every downstream portfolio and order dependency be migrated without
   silently changing strategy semantics or weakening safety controls?
4. Is a controlled, reversible migration safer than retaining the current
   legacy dependency?

The audit is a governance gate, not a performance comparison or optimization
exercise. Strong scores, favorable backtests, high shadow coverage, or a clean
current run cannot compensate for broken provenance, future leakage, unsafe
empty-state behavior, or an incomplete rollback plan.

# 2. Explicit Non-Production Boundary

The Phase 6B-7 audit is read-only with respect to production behavior. It may
inspect code, configurations, schemas, generated artifacts, test evidence, and
archived shadow results. It may produce audit tables and a readiness decision.
It must not connect a new source to an active consumer.

This design does not authorize:

- modification or migration of `portfolio_risk.py`;
- changes to `position_sizing.py`;
- changes to order draft, order review, or daily decision reporting;
- portfolio holdings, allocation, exposure, or risk-limit changes;
- order creation or brokerage interaction;
- scoring, FinalScore, BUY/WATCH thresholds, TradeSignal, or eligibility
  changes;
- RiskLevel or risk multiplier creation;
- replacement, deletion, or disabling of the legacy path;
- parameter optimization or selection from shadow performance; or
- treating `SHADOW_READY` as production approval.

All audit outputs must be labeled non-production. The safe outcome when
evidence is missing, conflicting, stale, or hard to explain is `NOT READY` and
no action—not an inferred PASS.

# 3. Legacy Portfolio Path Scope

The current legacy-controlled portfolio path is in audit scope from candidate
creation through reporting:

```text
backtest_engine.py / legacy backtest workflow
  -> results/backtest_qualified_20d.csv
  -> portfolio_risk.py
  -> results/model_portfolio.csv
  -> combined_scoring.py
  -> results/combined_score.csv
  -> position_sizing.py
  -> results/model_portfolio_sizing.csv
  -> order_draft.py
  -> results/order_draft.csv
  -> order_review.py
  -> results/order_review.csv
  -> portfolio action reporting
  -> daily_decision_report.py
```

The audit must inventory:

- every module and configuration value that reads or names
  `backtest_qualified_20d.csv`;
- every schema, validator, report, and test that requires BacktestScore,
  SharpeRatio, MaxDrawdown, legacy RiskLevel, or other backtest outcome fields;
- all implicit fallbacks to BacktestScore or legacy risk classifications;
- zero-row, missing-file, NaN, duplicate, and stale-artifact behavior;
- provenance currently propagated—or lost—between candidate, portfolio,
  sizing, order, and report boundaries; and
- research-only uses that may remain after production authority is removed.

Legacy research and backtesting may continue under explicit research labels.
The cutover target is removal of their authority over production candidate
admission, not deletion of historical research capability.

# 4. New Production Candidate Path Scope

The proposed new path is audited as a sequence of separate governed contracts:

```text
results/stock_rank.csv
  -> production_candidate_builder.py
  -> results/production_candidates.csv
  -> portfolio_candidate_adapter.py
  -> results/validated_portfolio_candidates.csv
  -> portfolio_risk_calculator.py
  -> results/portfolio_risk_inputs.csv
  -> shadow_validation.py
  -> results/shadow_validation_report.csv
```

The scope includes:

- production score and signal provenance copied into candidate artifacts;
- deterministic candidate ranking and RunId generation;
- candidate validation and BUY-plus-ELIGIBLE boundary enforcement;
- point-in-time market risk and pre-trade portfolio snapshot provenance;
- strict candidate/risk join behavior;
- READY/PENDING/BLOCKED and PASS/FAILED semantics;
- shadow classifications, coverage, unmatched rows, and failure reasons;
- stable zero-eligible and all-not-ready behavior; and
- evidence that no new-path component recalculates or promotes upstream
  scoring, signal, or eligibility decisions.

The audit boundary stops before active portfolio construction. A complete new
candidate/risk path is necessary but not sufficient for controlled migration.

# 5. Semantic Differences Between Legacy and Production Candidates

The two paths are not equivalent schemas for the same decision. Their meanings
must be compared explicitly rather than mapped by convenient column shape.

| Dimension | Legacy backtest candidate | Production candidate | Cutover treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governing question | Did a technical/backtest rule qualify historically? | Does the current production score and signal permit consideration now? | Preserve production meaning; do not reproduce legacy qualification |
| Source | `backtest_qualified_20d.csv` | `validated_portfolio_candidates.csv` | New candidate contract becomes the only admission source after approval |
| Row time semantics | Historical research summary, potentially full-sample | Explicit RunId and AsOfDate snapshot | Require point-in-time identity |
| Signal basis | Legacy technical filters/qualification | Current FinalScore and TradeSignal | Do not alias one to the other |
| Ranking | BacktestScore | CandidateRank ordered by FinalScore then Ticker | Use truthful production field names |
| Eligibility | IsQualified and legacy errors | BUY + source Eligibility + adapter validation | PortfolioEligible remains necessary, not sufficient |
| Performance fields | AverageReturn, WinRate, CAGR, trade counts, best/worst trade | Absent | Keep research-only; never required by production |
| Risk semantics | Legacy SharpeRatio, MaxDrawdown, RiskScore/RiskLevel from backtest outcomes | Separate point-in-time risk contract | Do not copy or rename legacy fields |
| Provenance | Limited/no unified run and model lineage | RunId, AsOfDate, ScoreModelVersion | Propagate exact provenance downstream |
| Missing data | May be embedded in Error or permissive fallbacks | Explicit ValidationStatus/Reason and fail-closed adapter | Preserve explicit no-action state |
| Portfolio context | Not a current pre-trade risk snapshot | PortfolioSnapshotId/PortfolioAsOfDate in risk layer | Keep eligibility separate from current portfolio risk |

The audit must reject mappings such as BacktestScore -> FinalScore,
SharpeRatio -> current volatility, completed-trade MaxDrawdown ->
TrailingDrawdown, or IsQualified -> risk-ready. Those pairs are semantically
different even when both are numeric or Boolean.

# 6. Candidate Contract Validation Criteria

Source of record: `results/validated_portfolio_candidates.csv`.

## Required identity and provenance

- Ticker is normalized, non-empty, and unique within the artifact.
- RunId is required and uniform for a single snapshot.
- AsOfDate is a valid canonical date and uniform for a single snapshot.
- ScoreModelVersion is required, deterministic, and uniform.
- CandidateRank is finite, positive, unique, and deterministic under
  FinalScore descending then Ticker ascending.

## Required candidate evidence

- FinalScore is present and finite; the adapter does not recalculate it.
- TradeSignal is a controlled production value and is preserved unchanged.
- Eligibility is a controlled upstream value and is preserved unchanged.
- PortfolioEligible is Boolean and can be true only for BUY plus ELIGIBLE.
- ValidationStatus and ValidationReason agree with eligibility and missing-data
  state.
- Confidence and approved score context, when carried by the upstream candidate
  contract, remain unchanged and finite under their source rules.

## Artifact and operational criteria

- Missing columns, duplicate normalized tickers, mixed metadata, stale source
  data, NaN, or infinity fail closed.
- The source candidate file is not modified by validators or shadow tools.
- Output ordering and RunId are reproducible for identical inputs.
- Zero candidates and zero PortfolioEligible candidates produce stable schemas
  and no promotion.
- No BacktestScore, forward return, legacy SharpeRatio, completed-trade
  MaxDrawdown, position, share, or order field is required.

Candidate contract PASS means “eligible for risk assessment under the current
signal boundary.” It never means safe to add to a portfolio.

# 7. Risk Contract Validation Criteria

Source of record: `results/portfolio_risk_inputs.csv`.

## Required identity and provenance

- Ticker, RunId, and AsOfDate exactly match the candidate composite key.
- ScoreModelVersion matches the joined candidate value.
- RiskModelVersion is present, deterministic, and uniform for its governed
  snapshot.
- PortfolioSnapshotId identifies an immutable pre-trade portfolio state.
- PortfolioAsOfDate is present for READY/PASS and is no later than AsOfDate.
- CalculationTimestamp is present but is never used as a market-data cutoff.
- ObservationEndDate is present for READY/PASS and is no later than AsOfDate.

## Required risk evidence

- Approved required fields—RiskObservationCount, Volatility20D,
  TrailingDrawdown, AverageVolume20D, AverageDollarVolume20D,
  LiquidityCoverage, and CurrentPortfolioWeight—are present, finite, and within
  their approved ranges for READY/PASS rows.
- Approved optional values such as Volatility60D and ATR14 are finite when
  emitted; their absence is handled according to the approved contract.
- RiskStatus is exactly READY, PENDING, or BLOCKED.
- RiskValidationStatus is exactly PASS or FAILED.
- READY/PASS is the only internally ready combination.
- PENDING/FAILED and BLOCKED/FAILED carry explicit reasons.
- Unknown or inconsistent combinations fail closed.

## Forbidden substitutions

- no BacktestScore;
- no legacy trade SharpeRatio;
- no completed-future-trade MaxDrawdown;
- no forward returns;
- no fabricated RiskLevel or risk multiplier; and
- no missing value replaced by zero, average, prior row, or candidate score.

Risk contract PASS means only that approved risk evidence is complete and
valid for the shadow boundary. It is not an allocation or order decision.

# 8. Point-in-Time Validation Requirements

For every candidate AsOfDate D:

1. Every market-derived risk observation must satisfy `Date <= D`.
2. Mutating all OHLCV values after D—including extreme, NaN, and infinity
   values—must not change the result at D.
3. Appending future OHLCV rows must not change the result at D.
4. ObservationEndDate must be no later than D.
5. PortfolioAsOfDate must be no later than D and must identify the portfolio
   state before the proposed trade.
6. The candidate universe, holdings, model metadata, and source facts must be
   those available at D; today's universe or portfolio cannot be substituted.
7. Joins use only exact Ticker + RunId + AsOfDate. No latest-row, fuzzy-date,
   forward-fill, backward-fill, or nearest-date repair is permitted.
8. Financial or fundamental data, if introduced in a future approved contract,
   must be gated by publication/availability date rather than reporting period
   end.
9. Re-running immutable inputs must reproduce all deterministic fields;
   CalculationTimestamp may differ and must be excluded from value equality.
10. No forward return or completed future trade outcome may enter a production
    candidate, risk, portfolio, sizing, or order decision.

Any leakage invariant violation is a blocking condition for the entire
affected snapshot. Other dates or high coverage cannot average it away.

# 9. Multi-Date Shadow Stability Checks

Readiness cannot be inferred from the current zero-eligible snapshot or from
synthetic unit tests alone. The audit requires immutable archived candidate,
risk, and shadow artifacts across multiple real AsOfDates and RunIds.

The reviewed set must include:

- zero eligible candidates;
- one eligible candidate;
- multiple simultaneous eligible candidates;
- READY/PASS, PENDING/FAILED, and BLOCKED/FAILED risk outcomes;
- missing risk, orphan risk, metadata mismatch, and duplicate-key failures;
- insufficient-history and stale-market-data cases;
- missing, stale, and conflicting portfolio snapshots;
- different market regimes when point-in-time archives permit; and
- an all-eligible-but-none-ready no-action state.

For each date, report:

- artifact paths or immutable identifiers and hashes;
- RunId, AsOfDate, ScoreModelVersion, RiskModelVersion, and portfolio snapshot
  identity;
- total candidates, eligible candidates, risk rows, exact matches, missing
  risk, orphan risk, and metadata mismatches;
- READY/PASS, PENDING/FAILED, BLOCKED/FAILED, unknown, and inconsistent status
  distributions;
- SHADOW_READY, SHADOW_PENDING, SHADOW_BLOCKED,
  SHADOW_MISSING_RISK, and SHADOW_METADATA_MISMATCH counts;
- exact-match and shadow-ready percentages with explicit denominators;
- failure reason counts and affected tickers; and
- leakage-test results for that snapshot.

Stability checks require:

1. deterministic row counts, ordering, classifications, and reasons for
   identical inputs;
2. no cross-date or cross-RunId joins;
3. stable header-only output for zero eligible candidates;
4. stable no-action behavior when every eligible row is pending or blocked;
5. no unexplained intermittent missing/orphan rows;
6. no unreviewed model-version drift during the observation set; and
7. documented coverage trends without optimizing the contract to improve
   them.

No universal minimum number of dates or coverage percentage is invented in
this design. Governance must approve an observation window and acceptance
criteria before the state can advance to controlled migration.

# 10. Blocking Conditions

Any of the following forces or retains `NOT READY`:

## Contract and provenance blockers

- missing required candidate or risk fields;
- duplicate normalized ticker or composite key;
- mixed or missing RunId, AsOfDate, ScoreModelVersion, or RiskModelVersion;
- candidate/risk ScoreModelVersion mismatch;
- stale or conflicting candidate, market, or portfolio snapshot metadata;
- orphan risk rows or missing eligible risk rows without an approved and
  explained operational cause;
- non-finite or out-of-range required numeric values; or
- unknown or inconsistent status combinations.

## Point-in-time blockers

- ObservationEndDate or PortfolioAsOfDate after candidate AsOfDate;
- future-price mutation or append changing a historical result;
- current universe, current holdings, future fundamentals, forward returns, or
  completed future trade outcomes entering an earlier decision;
- fuzzy/latest-date or Ticker-only fallback joins; or
- missing immutable evidence needed to prove historical isolation.

## Portfolio and order safety blockers

- zero-eligible or all-not-ready input can crash, divide by zero, create NaN
  weights, or produce an order;
- candidate eligibility can bypass risk readiness;
- missing price, risk, provenance, shares, or value can pass an order boundary;
- zero or non-integer shares can become an actionable order;
- empty orders are reported as approval rather than no action;
- source RunId/date/model versions are lost before order review/reporting;
- legacy backtest outcome fields remain necessary for production admission;
- no tested rollback/failback path exists; or
- shadow artifacts are consumed as production authorization.

## Governance blockers

- unresolved semantic mapping is hidden by renaming legacy fields;
- scope includes unapproved score, threshold, risk formula, allocation, sizing,
  or order-policy changes;
- multi-date evidence is insufficient or selected based on favorable results;
- test regressions, unreviewed warnings affecting safety, or undocumented
  exceptions remain; or
- no named human approval and rollback authority is defined.

A blocker cannot be waived by portfolio performance. Resolution requires
corrected evidence, repeated validation, and explicit human review.

# 11. Cutover Decision States

The audit produces exactly one state. States describe migration readiness, not
investment merit or permission to trade.

## NOT READY

Use when any blocking condition is present or required evidence is absent.

Permitted actions:

- continue research, contract correction, fixtures, and non-production tests;
- preserve the existing active path; and
- produce explicit no-action diagnostics.

Prohibited actions:

- production source switching;
- dual-write into active orders;
- portfolio or sizing migration; and
- interpreting partial coverage as approval.

## READY FOR SHADOW ONLY

Use when candidate and risk components validate independently and the shadow
framework is point-in-time safe, but real multi-date coverage, downstream
compatibility, operational stability, or rollback evidence remains incomplete.

Required evidence:

- candidate and risk schemas pass strict validation;
- exact-key shadow validation fails closed;
- required leakage and synthetic multi-date tests pass;
- zero-eligible and all-not-ready scenarios are safe; and
- no shadow output reaches production consumers.

Permitted actions are limited to continued parallel shadow generation,
archiving, comparison, and human review. This state does not authorize any
consumer change.

## READY FOR CONTROLLED MIGRATION

Use only when all `READY FOR SHADOW ONLY` requirements pass and the following
additional evidence is approved:

- a governance-approved multi-date real shadow observation window is complete;
- coverage, status distributions, failures, and leakage results meet
  predeclared acceptance criteria;
- every production consumer field has an explicit new source or approved
  removal treatment;
- legacy research metrics have no remaining authority over production
  admission;
- zero/all-pending/missing/invalid inputs are fail-closed through portfolio,
  sizing, order review, and reporting tests;
- exact provenance survives every proposed downstream boundary;
- controlled rollout scope, monitoring, stop conditions, ownership, rollback,
  and failback procedures are documented and tested;
- migration does not bundle score, threshold, allocation, risk-formula,
  position-sizing, or order-policy changes; and
- named human governance approval is recorded.

Even this state authorizes only a separately scoped controlled migration plan.
It does not itself switch production, approve a security, or approve an order.

## State transition rules

```text
NOT READY
  -> READY FOR SHADOW ONLY
       -> READY FOR CONTROLLED MIGRATION
```

- Advancement requires all evidence for the next state; no state may be
  skipped.
- Any new blocker immediately returns the audit to `NOT READY`.
- Model-version, contract, or material source changes invalidate prior evidence
  until revalidated.
- High returns, high FinalScore, or high SHADOW_READY coverage cannot override
  a blocker.
- In ambiguous cases, choose the lower-readiness state.

# 12. Audit Evidence and Decision Record

The future execution audit should preserve:

- repository branch, commit, PROJECT_VERSION, and audit timestamp;
- exact file hashes for source code, contracts, configurations, and artifacts;
- legacy and new dependency maps;
- candidate/risk schema validation results;
- multi-date shadow metrics and failure inventories;
- point-in-time/leakage evidence;
- full regression and boundary-test results;
- unresolved blockers with owner and required resolution;
- proposed rollout, monitoring, and rollback controls; and
- final state, rationale, approver, and approval date.

The decision record must distinguish observed facts, assumptions, open
questions, and human approvals. Missing evidence remains missing; it is not
converted into a favorable assumption.

# 13. Current Design-Level Assessment

Based on the architecture represented in Phases 6B-3 through 6B-6, the new
candidate, risk, and shadow components exist independently, while the active
portfolio path remains legacy-controlled. The current snapshot has zero
PortfolioEligible candidates, and synthetic multi-date tests demonstrate
shadow mechanics but do not constitute a real multi-date operational
observation window.

Accordingly, the design-level state is:

`READY FOR SHADOW ONLY`

This is not a final Phase 6B-7 execution-audit decision. Advancement to
`READY FOR CONTROLLED MIGRATION` requires the full evidence and human approval
defined above.

Design conclusion: cutover should proceed only when the system can explain
what it knows, prove what it did not know at each historical date, and stop
safely when either contract is incomplete. Until then, preserving cash and the
existing production boundary is the correct governance outcome.
