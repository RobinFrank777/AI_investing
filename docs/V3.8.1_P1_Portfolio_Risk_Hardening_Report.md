# V3.8.1 P1 Portfolio Risk Hardening Report

## 1. Objective

P1 hardens the capital-safety boundary in the production `run_portfolio.py` path. It ensures candidates pass numeric validity and risk readiness before they consume holding slots or receive allocation. It does not change security scoring, candidate thresholds, position sizing, or orders.

## 2. Previous Risks

The previous `portfolio_risk.py` implementation confirmed all targeted structural risks:

- `head(MAX_HOLDINGS)` ran before risk classification, so Unknown candidates occupied scarce slots while later valid candidates were ignored.
- Unknown mapped to multiplier zero, yet all selected multipliers were blindly divided by their sum. Empty, all-Unknown, or all-zero inputs could produce NaN/Inf.
- Missing/non-finite drawdown or Sharpe inputs were labeled Unknown only after selection.
- Allocation started from 80% as though it had to be filled, then applied a single-position `.clip()` without an explicit no-redistribution/cash contract.
- Empty/no-action conditions had no explicit portfolio status.
- Ranking ties had no stated stable secondary key.

## 3. Final Processing Order

```text
Qualified Candidate Artifact
→ Required Schema / Numeric Validity
→ Risk Classification and Readiness
→ Remove UNKNOWN / invalid / unusable rows
→ BacktestScore ranking (Ticker tie-break)
→ Select up to MAX_HOLDINGS
→ Remove non-finite or non-positive multipliers
→ Safe normalization
→ Single-position cap
→ Allocation invariants
```

The production input remains `results/backtest_qualified_20d.csv`, loaded through `load_qualified_candidates()`. The primary ranking authority remains descending `BacktestScore`; uppercase Ticker is a deterministic secondary key only.

## 4. Risk Readiness Contract

A candidate is risk ready only if:

- Ticker is non-empty;
- BacktestScore is numeric and finite;
- MaxDrawdown and SharpeRatio are numeric and finite;
- those risk inputs produce Low, Medium, or High rather than Unknown.

Existing multipliers remain unchanged: Low 1.00, Medium 0.80, High 0.50, Unknown 0.00. Unknown is not safe and is excluded before selection. A selected multiplier must additionally be finite and greater than zero before allocation.

## 5. Exposure Contract

- `MAX_HOLDINGS = 10`
- `MAX_TOTAL_EXPOSURE = 0.80`
- `MAX_SINGLE_POSITION_WEIGHT = 0.10`

80% is a cap, not a target. Valid results include any exposure from 0% through 80%. The base allocation uses risk multipliers against the 80% ceiling, then caps each position at 10%. No redistribution occurs after capping. Unused capacity remains cash / unallocated capital.

For example, three equal candidates each cap at 10%, yielding 30% exposure and 70% cash. This is intentional and safe.

## 6. Empty-State Contract

- `NO_QUALIFIED_CANDIDATES`: input has zero qualified rows.
- `NO_RISK_READY_CANDIDATES`: qualified rows exist, but none have usable Ticker, ranking score, and risk inputs.
- `NO_SIZABLE_POSITIONS`: risk-ready rows exist, but all allocation multipliers are non-finite or non-positive, or their denominator is unusable.
- `PORTFOLIO_READY`: one or more positions pass all boundaries.

Normal no-action conditions return a stable empty DataFrame with the portfolio output schema and a `PortfolioStatus` attribute. They do not raise or emit NaN allocations. Non-empty output also carries a `PortfolioStatus` column.

## 7. Numeric Safety

- NaN and ±Inf BacktestScore cannot enter the eligible ranking set.
- NaN and ±Inf risk inputs produce Unknown and are excluded before holding selection.
- NaN, ±Inf, zero, or negative multipliers cannot enter normalization.
- A non-finite or non-positive multiplier sum returns `NO_SIZABLE_POSITIONS`.
- Final weights are checked for finite, non-negative values, the 10% single cap, the 80% exposure cap with `1e-12` floating tolerance, and maximum holding count.

## 8. Position Cap Behavior

Redistribution after cap: **NO**.

P1 intentionally does not add an optimizer or iterative fill algorithm. Preserving cash is the correct result when the candidate set or per-position cap cannot safely use the full exposure ceiling.

## 9. Downstream Compatibility

`position_sizing.py` remains unchanged and compatible. The allocated output retains:

- Ticker
- BacktestScore
- RiskLevel
- RiskWeightMultiplier
- TargetWeight
- TargetWeightPercent
- PortfolioRole

The real current `backtest_qualified_20d.csv` was loaded through the hardened function without writing generated artifacts: 10 risk-ready rows were selected and total exposure remained within the 80% tolerance.

The repository also contains the newer `portfolio_candidate_adapter.py` / `portfolio_risk_calculator.py` metadata-rich risk path. It is not the input currently wired to `run_portfolio.py`; unifying those two generations is a future chain integration concern, not a reason to alter position sizing or order code during P1.

## 10. Tests

Focused coverage includes:

- one/multiple valid candidates;
- eligible populations above and below MAX_HOLDINGS;
- Unknown high-ranked candidates followed by valid candidates;
- empty, all-Unknown, and all-invalid candidate sets;
- NaN, ±Inf, negative, and zero multipliers;
- zero-denominator prevention;
- single-position and total-exposure caps;
- allowed cash after clipping;
- stable Ticker tie-break;
- input immutability and downstream column compatibility.
