# V3.8.1 T1 Threshold Governance Report

## 1. Objective

T1 governs the meaning and stability of the BUY threshold; it is not a parameter optimization exercise. The question is whether multiple dimensions provide enough evidence to replace the current threshold of 75 under the current Universe150 and score model.

## 2. Preconditions

- T0 decision: **VALID — NO CHANGE REQUIRED**
- Score model: `technical-score-v3.8.1-r1`
- Configured Universe: 150
- Historical eligible: 148
- Excluded only for history availability: SKHY and SPCX

Historical analysis eligibility is not Universe membership.

## 3. Current Threshold Authority

`trade_signal.py` is the production authority:

- WATCH: `FinalScore >= 60`
- BUY: `FinalScore >= 75` plus the existing volume, distance-to-high, Close/MA20, and MA20/MA60 conditions

`rank_stocks_v2.py` calls this authority; production candidate and report modules consume its resulting `TradeSignal`. `score_threshold_analysis.py` contains research-only grids and does not override production.

T1 simulations follow the requested governance contract `FinalScore >= candidate threshold`; they do not reuse the production 75 TradeSignal column. Therefore their “BUY observations” measure score-threshold supply before the additional production BUY conditions.

## 4. Historical Sample

- Date range: 2025-08-08 through 2026-08-12
- Cross-sectional dates: 254
- Eligible tickers: 148
- Valid score observations: 36,246
- 5D outcome samples: 35,506
- 20D outcome samples: 33,301
- 60D outcome samples: 27,421

Scores at date T use only data at or before T. Forward returns use ticker price at T+5, T+20, or T+60 trading rows. An unavailable horizon remains NaN rather than zero. This causes the expected sample-count decline at longer horizons and near the sample end.

## 6. Candidate Supply

Threshold 75 supplies 262 score-qualified observations from 33 tickers over 13 calendar months. Monthly counts range from zero to 50, with median 14. The zero months are July and the partial August 2026 endpoint, so they should not be interpreted independently as structural candidate absence.

Moving from 75 to 80 removes 57.6% of candidates and 48.5% of unique tickers. Moving from 75 to 70 more than doubles observations and adds 14 tickers. Both moves materially change the practical scarcity meaning of “BUY-quality score.”

There is no adjacent move that jointly improves supply, coverage, concentration, and all forward horizons. The 75→80 transition is the clearest unfavorable cliff: supply and breadth contract sharply, concentration nearly doubles, and forward medians do not improve.

## 10. Interpretation

- 80 is not supported: it creates scarce, concentrated candidates without forward-quality improvement.
- 70 increases supply and breadth, but its 20D median is lower than 75 and its 5D result remains negative. The evidence does not jointly establish that this wider band has better BUY semantics.
- 65 has balanced historical medians and broader coverage, but adopting it would fundamentally increase candidate frequency and overlap the present WATCH context. That requires more than retrospective comparative attractiveness.
- 75 has a weak short-horizon historical result and higher concentration than 70. These are governance limitations to monitor, not sufficient evidence of a better replacement cutoff given positive 20D/60D medians, horizon conflict, sample reduction, and continuity considerations.

The decision does not select the threshold with the highest historical return. It prioritizes continuity, interpretable scarcity, avoidance of concentrated high-threshold samples, and the absence of multidimensional evidence for change.

## 11. Governance Decision

**KEEP BUY_THRESHOLD = 75**

Reason: **insufficient evidence to justify threshold change**. Neighboring alternatives present conflicting benefits and costs; none improves candidate supply, temporal stability, cross-ticker coverage, forward quality, and concentration together.
