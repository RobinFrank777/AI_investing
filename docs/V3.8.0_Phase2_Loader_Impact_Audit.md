## 11. Findings by Severity

### Critical

- Historical loader-based V3.8.0 ranking, backtest, portfolio, sizing, order,
  daily decision, stock-card, and Terminal artifacts produced before commit
  `6596524` are stale/invalid and must not be used as the new baseline.

### High

- The corrected loader materially changed all 19 backtest summaries, 11 ranks,
  3 stances, qualification count (12 to 10), and portfolio membership (CAKE to
  INTC).

### Medium

- Universe/data/processed counts differ (19 configured daily symbols, 162 local
  OHLCV files, and 150 processed research symbols); reports must disclose the
  relevant scope.
- Live-data update was not part of the deterministic audit and requires a
  separately labeled run if desired.

### Low

- Direct raw-CSV consumers duplicate some loading behavior, although the active
  Universe150 path was verified unaffected.

No evidence was found that incorrect prices still propagate through the
corrected 19-symbol production rebuild, that a major active pipeline still swaps
Open/Close, or that qualitative profiles changed.
