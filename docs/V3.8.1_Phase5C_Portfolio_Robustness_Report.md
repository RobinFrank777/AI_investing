# AI_investing V3.8.1 Phase 5C Portfolio Robustness Report

## Methodology

Phase 5C tested the unchanged Phase 5B-2 research portfolio against:

1. SPY and QQQ normalized Close-price benchmarks on exact common dates;
2. signal-Close versus next-trading-day-Open execution;
3. 0, 10, 25, and 50 bps costs per side; and
4. realized P/L attribution by ticker.

No score, signal, threshold, holding period, production module, or portfolio
parameter was optimized or selected based on performance.

## Findings

### Benchmark

Through the common 2026-08-05 endpoint, AI returned 20.98%, versus 16.47% for
SPY and 21.23% for QQQ. AI drawdown (-11.77%) was between SPY (-9.13%) and QQQ
(-12.19%). The result resembles QQQ more than it establishes benchmark
superiority, and exposure/risk are not matched.

### Execution

Next-day Open produced 22.70% total return versus 21.24% at signal Close, with
20 instead of 21 trades. Close execution is therefore not the sole source of
the baseline result in this sample. The finding is fragile because no spreads,
slippage, or live fill validation exists.

### Costs

Performance degraded monotonically as costs increased. Total return remained
18.11% at 50 bps per side, while Sharpe declined from 1.42 to 1.25 and maximum
drawdown worsened from -11.77% to -12.24%.

### Contribution

Performance is not broad. MU contributed 54.11% of net P/L; MU plus SNDK
contributed 80.96%. The top three positive contributors exceeded 100% because
INTC and GOOGL detracted. This concentration is the strongest robustness
warning found.

## Limitations

- current-universe survivorship bias;
- no point-in-time universe;
- limited historical regime of roughly one year;
- no live execution validation;
- no tax impact;
- no short side;
- no dividends in benchmark returns;
- no spread, market-impact, or liquidity model;
- equal-slot sizing and fixed 20-day exits remain assumptions;
- low average invested exposure and no exposure-matched benchmark;
- stale local SPY/QQQ data truncates benchmark comparison to 2026-08-05; and
- only 21 portfolio trades across six tickers.

## Recommendation

**ADDITIONAL VALIDATION REQUIRED**

The simulation survives the specified execution and cost diagnostics, and its
accounting logic remains coherent. It is not ready for model-governance approval
because return contribution is highly concentrated, the universe is
survivorship-biased, and the regime/trade sample is small.

Before governance review, validate on a point-in-time universe and a genuinely
out-of-sample period, add live-realistic spreads/slippage and dividends, and
compare risk on exposure-matched terms. Do not tune the current model against
these results.

The Buffett-oriented risk lens influenced this conclusion: robustness is about
surviving adverse assumptions and avoiding dependence on a few lucky outcomes,
not merely retaining a positive backtest return.
