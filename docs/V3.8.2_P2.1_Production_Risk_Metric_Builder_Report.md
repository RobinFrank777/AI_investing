# AI_investing V3.8.2 P2.1 Production Risk Metric Builder Report

## Objective

P2.1 establishes the production, point-in-time-safe bridge from the canonical
production candidate artifact to `results/portfolio_risk_inputs.csv`. It does
not connect that artifact to the portfolio allocator; allocator cutover remains
P2.2 work.

## Input Authority

The sole candidate input is `results/production_candidates.csv`, read through
`portfolio_candidate_adapter.load_production_candidates()` and validated by
`build_validated_portfolio_candidates()`. Eligibility remains exactly:

`TradeSignal == BUY AND Eligibility == ELIGIBLE`

WATCH and IGNORE rows never receive production allocation authority. The risk
builder reads no qualified-backtest list, forward-return output, or legacy BUY
list.

## Output Artifact

The single canonical output remains:

`results/portfolio_risk_inputs.csv`

The producer is
`portfolio_risk_calculator.build_production_risk_inputs()`. A header-only
artifact is valid when no portfolio-eligible candidates exist.

## Point-in-Time Contract

Market rows are filtered to `Date <= Candidate.AsOfDate` before the trailing
window is selected. `LatestClose` is the final close inside that filtered
window, and `LatestCloseAsOf` is its date. Thus
`LatestCloseAsOf <= Candidate.AsOfDate` is enforced by construction.

Tests verify that appending future rows or mutating future rows leaves the
result unchanged. This supplies a safe downstream price field without changing
position-sizing policy in P2.1.

## Historical Window / Staleness

- Required observations: 60
- Effective lookback: trailing 60 available market observations through AsOfDate
- Maximum calendar staleness: 5 days
- Volatility: daily close returns, sample standard deviation, annualized by
  `sqrt(252)`
- SharpeRatio: mean daily return divided by sample daily-return standard
  deviation, annualized by `sqrt(252)`, risk-free rate assumed zero
- MaxDrawdown: minimum of `Close / cumulative_max(Close) - 1` in the 60-row
  window; zero is best and drawdowns are negative
- Zero or non-finite volatility: `INVALID_RISK_METRIC`, never risk-ready

## RiskModelVersion

The one canonical version is:

`risk-model-v3.8.2-p2`

It identifies the window, drawdown and Sharpe formulas, established P1 risk
levels, and readiness contract. It does not version portfolio exposure caps or
allocation policy.

## Metadata Preservation

RunId, AsOfDate, UniverseVersion, and ScoreModelVersion must each be present and
single-valued. They are copied exactly into every risk row. Mixed metadata is
rejected before any per-ticker calculation. RiskModelVersion is supplied only
by the risk builder.

## UniverseVersion Fix

Previously, the portfolio candidate adapter projected UniverseVersion out of
its validated artifact. Its source/output schemas now preserve the field.
Validation requires an exact match to `config.PRIMARY_UNIVERSE_VERSION`:

- present and matching: allowed and preserved
- missing: rejected
- mismatched: rejected as incompatible
- mixed: rejected as incompatible

CSV save/read round trips retain UniverseVersion.

## Risk Readiness Semantics

Overall build states:

- `RISK_INPUTS_READY`: at least one eligible candidate is risk-ready
- `NO_PORTFOLIO_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATES`: normal no-action, stable empty schema
- `NO_RISK_READY_CANDIDATES`: eligible candidates exist but none has sufficient
  valid risk evidence

Row states:

- `RISK_READY`
- `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`
- `STALE_HISTORY`
- `INVALID_RISK_METRIC`
- `INVALID_PRICE`

Partial readiness retains every eligible row and its diagnostic status. No
unready row is defaulted to Low risk. Unknown/unready risk has multiplier 0.
Metadata incompatibility fails the build before output rather than producing a
partially trusted artifact.

## Risk Level and Multiplier

The P1 policy is unchanged:

- Low: MaxDrawdown >= -0.10 and SharpeRatio >= 2; multiplier 1.00
- Medium: MaxDrawdown >= -0.25 and SharpeRatio >= 1; multiplier 0.80
- High: other finite metrics; multiplier 0.50
- Unknown: unusable metrics; multiplier 0.00 and not ready

## Score Field / BacktestScore Compatibility

FinalScore remains production score authority. CandidateRank then Ticker are
the deterministic candidate-context fields. To keep the output consumable for
the future P2.2 migration, `BacktestScore = FinalScore` is emitted only as a
compatibility alias, with `BacktestScoreSemantic = COMPATIBILITY_ALIAS_ONLY`.
No backtest artifact or forward-return metric supplies this value. The alias is
deprecated and should be removed after downstream migration to FinalScore.

## Legacy AverageReturn / WinRate Treatment

AverageReturn and WinRate remain nullable compatibility columns. They are not
calculated, zero-filled, fabricated, or required for RiskReadyForPortfolio.
P2.2 should remove them from the active production-required allocator schema.

## No-Legacy-Authority Proof

The builder's executable input path is limited to:

1. production candidate CSV through its formal reader/validator;
2. per-ticker historical market price files through `stock_loader.load_stock`;
3. canonical configuration constants.

It contains no read of `backtest_qualified_20d.csv`, backtest outputs,
forward-return diagnostics, or a legacy candidate list. Removing or hiding
those artifacts cannot affect candidate-to-risk production results.

## Current Production Behavior

The builder can now create the canonical risk-input artifact, including a safe
latest close and readiness evidence. The active `portfolio_risk.py` default
reader and allocation behavior are unchanged: an eligible production candidate
still returns `PRODUCTION_RISK_INPUTS_NOT_READY_NO_ACTION`. P2.1 performs no
allocation.

## Deferred to P2.2

- Read and validate `portfolio_risk_inputs.csv` in the active allocator.
- Admit only `RiskReadyForPortfolio == True` rows.
- Rank using FinalScore, CandidateRank, and Ticker rather than BacktestScore.
- Remove AverageReturn and WinRate from the active required schema.
- Consume LatestClose/LatestCloseAsOf downstream without reopening future-data
  access.
- Preserve the existing caps, holdings limit, and allocation policy.
