# AI_investing V3.8.1 Phase 5C Benchmark Comparison Report

Report date: 2026-08-12

## Methodology

The Phase 5B-2 equity curve and SPY/QQQ canonical Close series are normalized to
$100,000 on the first portfolio date. No dividends, costs, taxes, or benchmark
tradability adjustments are included.

The portfolio ends on 2026-08-11, but both local benchmark files end on
2026-08-05. No values were filled or invented. The exact common comparison
period is therefore 2025-09-15 through 2026-08-05 (224 trading rows).

## Results

| Series | Cumulative return | Maximum drawdown |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| AI portfolio | 20.98% | -11.77% |
| SPY | 16.47% | -9.13% |
| QQQ | 21.23% | -12.19% |

AI finished 4.51 percentage points above SPY and 0.25 points below QQQ on this
specific common period. Its maximum drawdown was worse than SPY and slightly
better than QQQ.

## Limitations

- The sample covers less than one year and one market regime.
- Benchmarks use price returns, not total returns with dividends.
- AI assumptions exclude costs/slippage in this comparison.
- The AI universe is current and survivorship-biased; benchmarks are not matched
  to its constituent or sector risk.
- AI averaged only 18.28% invested, so risk and return are not exposure-matched.
- Local benchmark data is stale by four trading dates versus the full portfolio
  curve.

No superiority claim is supported by this sample.
